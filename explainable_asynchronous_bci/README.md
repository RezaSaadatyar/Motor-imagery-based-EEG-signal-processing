# Explainable AI and Asynchronous Motor Imagery BCI

**Status:** Exploratory / planned — this combination may be split into independent studies after feasibility review.

## Research directions

1. **Explainability and neurophysiological interpretation:** Examine model behaviour using appropriate spatial, spectral and attribution analyses, without equating a visual explanation with causal or clinical validation.
2. **Asynchronous or continuous decoding:** Investigate sliding-window inference, idle-state detection, calibration and timing in a carefully described offline simulation.

## Feasibility requirements

Explainability requires trained, evaluated models and suitable signal metadata. Asynchronous decoding requires recordings and labels that support the proposed timing and idle-state protocol; offline windows are not evidence of working real-time BCI control.

## Intended outputs

Evidence-based explanation analyses and, if supported by the data, a reproducible offline streaming simulation. These remain future directions.

## Next stage

Decide whether these are scientifically coherent as one study or should become separate subprojects, then verify dataset suitability.
